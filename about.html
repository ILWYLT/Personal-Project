ABOUT US

About Us

Who are we?

We are a collective of mages, trained under the Mage Guild, who operate and maintain the summoning portals between worlds. Our agency began when the founder grew tired of ferrying friends — and friends of friends — between worlds by hand, so he built the portals and invited the Mage Guild to help him turn it into a service.

Our Founder

The founder of this travel agency is a dark mage (don't worry, dark mages are safe here) who can travel through dimensions and can enter any dimension at any time, in any timeline. He also knows a great many elements, and has now retired to live peacefully somewhere in the world. No one really knows where he is — he literally can be anywhere...

Our Mages

Our mages are a mix of new students who need to expand their mana pool and some professional teachers to oversee the students and step in if the ritual needs more mana to succeed. There are enough failsafes that even if the summoning sequence fails, there will not be any consequences. If you want to know more, details are on the Summoning Ritual page.

Our Goal

Our mission is to bring more people from the modern world into the isekai world. Whether you wish to settle down and start a new life, take part in a hero's journey to slay the Demon King, or simply visit for several days of travel and sightseeing — we make it happen.

History and Lore

How it begins

Isekai Travels was originally created by the strongest dark mage in our world. He managed to break the 4th-wall and gained the ability to travel across dimensions. He took several people he met to the modern world, and took people from the modern world back to his own world.

But after some time, the demand grew bigger, and the dark mage was busy with other life stuff, so he simply created some summoning portals and assigned some teachers and recruited students to help.

More details on the Summoning Ritual page.

When was it found?

Well, given that our founder can travel through time and space, there isn't a typical "founded year." But in our world, the first 4th-wall break happened about 5 years after the Great War in the fantasy world...

Hope that satisfied you.

The isekai world

Our world is currently a peaceful world.

After the Great War, the kingdoms have settled into a stable situation. There are still dungeons, monsters, and even minor threats like bandits and pirates, but nothing major is happening in the world.

The Hero's Path is also just an artificial experience created by our dedicated mages, there will not be actual threats harming to the world.

Summon Ritual

Magic

The founder has created a spell circle to summon people from the modern world to our fantasy world.

There are two types of summoning circles:

One is pure summoning. It's the more cost-efficient plan, perfect for people who are just coming over to sightsee, free roam, or move in.

The other is a Hero's summoning circle. People summoned by this spell circle automatically gain great powers, and they return to the modern world once they've defeated the Demon Lord. Perfect for those who want a more traditional isekai summon.

Summoning Circle Magic System

If you're REALLY curious about how the spell circle works, here's how the magic works to bring a living being from one world to another.

First of all, the dark mage created these passages that connect the modern world to the fantasy world. All the mages need to do is channel mana into the spell circle. Then the spell circle will start to pull or push the people from one end of the portal to the other end. It kind of works similarly to a drinking straw.

As for the Hero's summoning circle, there will be a small enhancement during the transition. Before the people are transported, they temporarily go into spirit form so the enhancement can directly apply to the soul. Then it quickly reattaches the soul back to the body, and you will get your hero abilities as you are summoned into our world. All of this is already drawn in the spell circle, and our mages just need to make sure they channel the mana into it, and it should work just fine.

Services

General

We provide basic weapon and magic training, convert dollars into coins, and provide transportational teleportation in both the fantasy world AND the real world.

Sightsee

Our world is full of interesting sights to behold. Our Sightseeing tour will also cover any transport fees, inn rooms, and meals, so you can focus more on the sights, people, and experiences, rather than worrying about survival and money.

Hero's Path

If you wish to have the more traditional isekai summon, then we can also summon you to our world. Our staff will still run the traditional routine and have you experience the hero's journey in defeating the Demon Lord. Don't worry about being defeated and dying — we also have failsafe insurance to make sure you always come back in one piece.

Free Roam

If you wish to sightsee on your own instead of following a tour guide, then you can always choose Free Roam. You'll get a card that will give you 3 free meals and 1 inn stay per day along with the package. Just beware — if you die, there's a penalty fee for bringing you back to life.

Migration

If you want to permanently move to the Isekai world, we will still provide some starter equipment and money to get you started in this world. And of course, if you wish to travel back to your original world, there's a 7-day return allowance, so if you find the lives living in Isekai to not be your thing, you can always return to us within 7 days and we will send you back to your original world for free. But your death will be permanent, so travel at your own risk.

FAQ

1. How to actually apply for a trip?

A: There's no reservation needed. Just come to one of our shops, and our staff will immediately arrange the summoning ritual for you within 15 minutes.

2. How many people can we bring?

A: If it's a sightseeing tour, then it can be anywhere from 5 to 30 people per trip. If it's a Hero's tour, then it's a maximum of 4 people in a party. Pets are also allowed. Free Roam and Migration can be as minimal as 1 person.

3. What language do they use?

A: Thanks to the summoning portals, there will be no language gaps. Our base language is English, for both talking and writing. So you do not need to learn any new languages.

4. Is it only teenagers who can apply for the Hero's Path?

A: No, anyone can participate in the Hero's Path package. But given that the adventure might be a bit difficult and dangerous, we generally recommend it for people under the age of 30.

5. How do I open the Status Screen?

A: Unfortunately, there is no built-in Status Screen for our world. Only the ones choosing the Hero's Path can open Status Screens.

6. What's the money to coins ratio?

A: Please check with our staff to acquire the latest money-to-gold ratio.
