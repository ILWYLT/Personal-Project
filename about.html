ABOUT US

Who are we?

We are a bunch of mages trained under the mage guide to perfect the art of summoning magic.

Our Goal

Our mission is to bring more people from the modern world to the Isekai World. Whether to stay and live there, wanting to take part of the hero's journey to slay the demon king, or just visit there for serveral days of travel and sightsee.

How it begins

Iskeai Travels is originally created by our strongest dark mage in our world. He managed to break the 4th wall and able to travel to the multi-dimension, and took serveral people he met to the modern world, and took people from modern worlds back to his own world.

When is it found?

Well, given how our founder can travel through time and space? There isn't a typical "founded year". But in the our world, the 4th wall break happened about 5 years after The Great War happened in the fantasy world... Hope that satisfied you.

Magic

The founder has created a spell circle to summon people from the modern world to our fantasy world.

There's two types of summoning circles. One is just pure summoning. Perfect for people who's just coming over to sightsee or to move in. The other is a hero's summoning circle. People summoned by this spell circle will automatically be gifted great powers, and they will return to the modern world when they defeated the demon lord. Perfect for those who wanted a more tradition Isekai summon.

Summoning Circle Magic System

If you're REALLY curious about how the spell circle works, here's how the magic works to bring a living being from one world to another.

First of all, the dark mage first created these passage that's connecting the modern world to the fantasy world. And all the mages need to do is to channel mana into the spell circle. Then the spell circle will starts to pull or push the people from one end of the portal, to the other end. Kind of works similarly to a drinking straw.

As for the hero summoning circle, there will be a small enchancement during the transition. Before the people is transported, they will temporarily go into spirit form, so the enchancement can directly apply to the soul, and then quickly reattach it back to the body, and you will get your hero abilities as you are summoned into our world. All of which is already drawn in the spell circle, and our mages just need to make sure to channel the mana into the spell circle, and it should work just fine.
