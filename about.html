ABOUT US

About Us

Who are we?

We are a bunch of mages trained under the mage guide to perfect the art of summoning magic.

Our Goal

Our mission is to bring more people from the modern world to the Isekai World. Whether to stay and live there, wanting to take part of the hero's journey to slay the demon king, or just visit there for serveral days of travel and sightsee.

History

How it begins

Iskeai Travels is originally created by our strongest dark mage in our world. He managed to break the 4th wall and able to travel to the multi-dimension, and took serveral people he met to the modern world, and took people from modern worlds back to his own world.

When is it found?

Well, given how our founder can travel through time and space? There isn't a typical "founded year". But in the our world, the 4th wall break happened about 5 years after The Great War happened in the fantasy world... Hope that satisfied you.

Summon Ritual

Magic

The founder has created a spell circle to summon people from the modern world to our fantasy world.

There's two types of summoning circles:

One is just pure summoning. The more cost efficent plan. Perfect for people who are just coming over to sightsee, free roam, or to move in.

The other is a hero's summoning circle. People summoned by this spell circle automatically gain great powers, and they will return to the modern world when they defeated the demon lord. Perfect for those who wanted a more tradition Isekai summon.

Summoning Circle Magic System

If you're REALLY curious about how the spell circle works, here's how the magic works to bring a living being from one world to another.

First of all, the dark mage first created these passages that's connecting the modern world to the fantasy world. And all the mages need to do is to channel mana into the spell circle. Then the spell circle will start to pull or push the people from one end of the portal, to the other end. Kind of works similarly to a drinking straw.

As for the hero summoning circle, there will be a small enchancement during the transition. Before the people is transported, they will temporarily go into spirit form, so the enchancement can directly apply to the soul, and then quickly reattach it back to the body, and you will get your hero abilities as you are summoned into our world. All of which is already drawn in the spell circle, and our mages just need to make sure to channel the mana into the spell circle, and it should work just fine.

Services

General

We provide basic weapon and magic training, convert dollars into coins, and transportational teleportation in both fantasy world AND real world.

Sightsee

Our world is full of interesting sights to behold. Our Sightseeing tour will also cover any transport fee, inn rooms, and meals, so you can focus more on the sights, people and experiences, rather than worrying about survival and money.

Hero Summon

If you wish to have the more traditional Isekai Summon, then we can also summon you to our world, our staff will still run the traditional routine and have you experience the hero's journey in defeating the demon king. Don't worry about being defeated and dying, we also have fail save ensurance to make sure you always come back in one piece.

Free Roam

If you wish to sightsee on your own instead of following a tour guide, then you can always choose free roam. You'll get a card that will give you 3 free meals and 1 inn stay per day along with the package. Just beware — if you die, there's a penalty fee for bringing you back to life.

Migration

If you want to permanently move to the Isekai world, we will still provide some starter equipment and money to get you started in this world. And of course, if you wish to travel back to your original world, there's a 7-day return allowance, so if you find the lives living in Isekai to not be your thing, you can always return to us within 7 days and we will send you back to your original world for free. But your death will be permanent, so travel at your own risk.

FAQ
