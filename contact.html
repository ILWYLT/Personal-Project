FAQ:

Q1. How much will be the cost for revival?

A: It varies depending on death location and rescue difficulty. If it's near civilization, it should be around two gold coins. But if you die in some dangerous place like the 100th floor of a dungeon, right inside some dragon's nest, or as a prisoner in a place that's hard to reach, then it will be more expensive.

Q2. Can I request a refund if something wrong happens?

A: With a valid reason, you can visit our shop and we will help with the issue you are facing or provide a 50% refund.

Q3. What if I run out of gold while I'm traveling?

A: You can either go to the Adventurer Guild to do quests and get more silver and gold coins, or you can simply look for our staff, and they can always provide cash-to-coins exchange.

Q4. How do I contact your staff during my trip?

A: For Sightsee, Hero's Path, and Free Roam, we will hand out a unique spell card that acts as a protective charm and a communication device to our staff. We will give a quick tutorial on how to use the card before the trip begins. For Migration, you have to contact us at our shop front in the isekai world.

Q5. Can I change my service after I've already arrived?

A: For Sightsee and Hero's Path, you can only end the tour early, and we will give the corresponding refund depending on how deep your journey has gone. For Free Roam and Migration, you can change from one to the other, but you have to pay the extra fee along with the extra benefits you will get or have used.
